## Featured Technical Domains

### 🛢️ Geoscience & Energy

My geoscience experience covers **structural geology, 2D/3D seismic interpretation, regional exploration, petroleum systems, well-log correlation, geological mapping, and subsurface modeling**.

- **Exploration & subsurface:** Basin-scale interpretation, structural frameworks, reservoir correlation, prospect and portfolio evaluation
- **Data & digital workflows:** Well and seismic data management, geospatial integration, technical visualization, and quality control
- **Tools:** Petrel · Techlog · IP · ArcGIS · Google Earth
- **Future portfolio examples:** Public/sample-data well visualization, stratigraphic correlation, GIS workflows, and geoscience analytics

### 📈 Financial Markets & Investment Analytics

I analyze **gold and equity markets**, combining **technical and fundamental perspectives**:

- **Price analysis:** Trends, market structure, support/resistance, Fibonacci retracements and extensions
- **Time analysis:** Historical timing relationships, cycles, and scenario studies
- **Volume analysis:** Volume-price behavior and participation confirmation
- **Fundamental analysis:** Business performance, financial statements, economic conditions, and valuation context
- **Software opportunities:** OHLCV data pipelines, analytical dashboards, comparison tools, charting applications, and market-data validation

*Financial-market work shared here is for research, technical demonstration, and education—not guaranteed investment outcomes or personalized investment advice.*

### 🏗️ Construction, Automotive & Facility Operations

My construction and project-management background includes **steel structures, mechanical installations, industrial works, procurement, cost control, project execution, and client coordination**, including work in **automotive manufacturing facilities**.

I am applying this domain knowledge to **facility-management software**, operational data structures, maintenance workflows, and performance reporting.
